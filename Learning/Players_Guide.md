# Player's Guide

What the Outposts Expanded patch does in your game, mechanic by mechanic. For the code side, see the [Developer's Guide](Developers_Guide). Everything below assumes both [Regions and Societies](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784666060) and [Vanilla Outposts Expanded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2688941031) are installed alongside this patch.

---

## Outposts count toward territory

With the patch installed, every Vanilla Outposts Expanded outpost on the world map — yours and the AI's — is recognised by Regions and Societies as an **outpost** under its territory rules. Each one reports its real occupant count as population, so a staffed mining outpost genuinely weighs into who holds a province, its demographics, and the ownership ladder, instead of counting for nothing. Empty or fuller, the numbers you see in the region readouts track the pawns actually stationed there.

## Placement respects regional ownership

When you send a caravan to establish a VOE outpost, the tile now has to pass Regions and Societies' placement rules as well as VOE's own checks. If territory governance refuses the tile — for example, the ground is held too firmly by another faction — VOE's establishment interface shows the refusal with the same reason text Regions and Societies gives elsewhere, so you know *why* the spot is off-limits, not just that it is.

This gate only ever adds refusals with a stated reason; everywhere Regions and Societies allows, VOE behaves exactly as it always did.

## World generation seeds real outposts

On a newly generated world, AI factions receive VOE outposts seeded around their settlements, up to each territory's tier-based allowance (a core mechanic — see the core [Player's Guide](https://github.com/Regions-and-societies/Core-MMF/wiki/Players_Guide)). The patch is what makes those outposts *real* VOE outposts:

- **The type fits the terrain** — mining in hills, logging in forest, farming and hunting where those make sense, with a plain encampment as the fallback.
- **The name fits the faction** — generated by the faction's own settlement name-maker.
- **They are staffed** — each seeded outpost starts with a generated member of its faction, so it is a living site, not an empty marker.

Without this patch, core cannot build VOE outposts and a generated world carries only settlements.

## No settings of its own

Installed means enabled. All governance toggles — including the master switch that turns seeding and territory rules off at once — live in the **Regions and Societies** mod settings, not here.

## Troubleshooting

- On a healthy load the log shows one startup line: `[RegionsAndSocieties.VOECP] Registered the Vanilla Outposts Expanded adapter and outpost creator (priority 110).`
- If your VOE version lacks the establishment check this patch hooks, you'll see a one-line warning and the placement gate is skipped — everything else keeps working.
- In dev mode, the debug action **R&S VOE-CP: world-object dump** (under *Regions and Societies*) lists every VOE outpost with its classification, population and owner, which is the quickest way to confirm the patch is doing its job.
