[h1]Regions and Societies: Outposts Expanded Patch[/h1]
Compatibility patch: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2688941031]Vanilla Outposts Expanded[/url] outposts are classified, populated and owned under [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784666060]Regions and Societies[/url] territory rules; world generation seeds VOE outposts around settlements; and outpost placement respects regional ownership.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Typed outpost binding:[/b] outposts classify under territory rules and report their real occupant count as population - they weigh into ownership and demographics instead of reading zero.
[*] [b]Placement respects ownership:[/b] when Regions and Societies refuses a tile, VOE's own placement check refuses it too, with the reason string shown right in VOE's UI.
[*] [b]Worldgen outpost seeding:[/b] AI factions get outposts seeded around their settlements - the def picked by archetype (mining, logging, farming, hunting), the name from the faction's own name-maker, each staffed with a generated pawn.
[/list]

No settings of its own - all governance toggles live in Regions and Societies. Installed means enabled.
